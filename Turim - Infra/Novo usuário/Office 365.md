# Atribuir licença
[Microsoft 365 admin center](https://admin.cloud.microsoft/#/homepage)

Microsoft 365 admin center > Users > Active users > Selecionar usuário > Licenses and apps > Microsoft 365 Business Premium (Standard para sala de reunião)

# Incluir grupo TodosTurim
[Microsoft 365 admin center](https://admin.cloud.microsoft/#/homepage)

Microsoft 365 admin center > Teams & groups > Active teams & groups > Distribution list > Selecionar o grupo Todos Turim > Members > View all and manage members > Add members > selecionar usuário


# Retenção (Litigio)
https://admin.cloud.microsoft/exchange?#/homepage

Exchange admin center > Recipients > Mailboxes > Selecioner usuário > Others > Manage litigation hold > Litigation hold On
